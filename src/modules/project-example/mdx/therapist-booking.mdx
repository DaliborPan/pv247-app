## Roles

- **Visitor** - browses the public website (home, pricing, blog) and can get in touch via the contact form
- **Client** - signs in with an SSO, books and cancels sessions, sees their upcoming and past bookings
- **Therapist (admin)** - manages time slots, bookings, clients, prices and website content

## Bookings

- The therapist publishes availability time blocks; the client picks a time within them
- The client chooses a session type (each has its own price) and a format: in person / online
- States: pending → confirmed → completed, or cancelled
- The client can cancel no later than 48 hours before the start
- The therapist confirms, cancels and reschedules bookings and can also create them (including for a client without an account)
- A video call link is generated for online sessions

## Notifications

- Therapist: e-mail about a new booking and about a cancellation by the client
- Client: e-mail about a booking being confirmed, cancelled or rescheduled

## Payments

- The therapist sets bank details and session type prices
- The client sees the payment details and a QR code for a bank transfer with the booking (no online payment)

## Client management

- Client list and client detail with session history
- Dated private notes about clients

## Calendar

- Calendar overview of sessions
- Export of bookings as a calendar feed (e.g. to subscribe to in Google Calendar)

## Website content

- Blog with categories and a setting for which articles are public
